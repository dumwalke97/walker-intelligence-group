Privacy policy

Walker Intelligence Group ("we", "us") runs walkerig.com. This page explains what information we collect on this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and in plain English.

Information you give us

When you fill out a contact form, we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, company, and message. Forms are delivered to our email by FormSubmit, a third-party form service. We use this information only to respond to you and to provide the services you ask about.

When you email or call us, we keep that correspondence so we can follow up and serve you.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, for example which pages are viewed, how people found us, and whether a contact form was completed. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your browser type, device, approximate location, and pages visited. It does not give us your name or contact details.

You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and our business, such as our website host, form service, and analytics provider, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information you have sent us. Just email dustin@walkerig.com or call 317-531-7681.

This website is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
