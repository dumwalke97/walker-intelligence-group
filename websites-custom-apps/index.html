Websites & Custom Apps

Websites and custom apps that work for your business.

A website that brings in leads, and custom tools built around the way you actually work. We design it, build it, host it, and keep it updated.

The problem we solve

A lot of small business websites look fine but do nothing. They load slowly, do not show up in search, and make it hard for a customer to reach you. Off-the-shelf software has the same problem. It almost fits, so your team works around it every day.

We build websites that are fast, easy to find on Google, and designed to turn visitors into calls and form submissions. When software does not fit, we build the tool you actually need.

What we build for you

Lead-generating websites

Clean, fast sites with clear calls to action, search engine basics done right, and contact forms that reach you.

Client portals and online forms

Give customers a place to submit requests, upload files, sign documents, or check status without calling you.

Dashboards

See the numbers that matter in one place, pulled live from the systems you already use.

Custom internal tools

Small, focused apps built around your exact process, replacing the spreadsheet that has grown out of control.

Common projects

  • A new website for a service business with online quote requests
  • A customer portal for uploading documents and tracking job status
  • An owner dashboard with sales, cash, and pipeline in one view
  • A scheduling or inventory tool that replaces a shared spreadsheet

Questions we hear

Do you handle hosting and updates?

Yes. We host what we build and handle updates, security, and backups, so you do not have to manage another vendor.

Can you redo our existing website?

Yes. We can rebuild it, improve it, or move it to faster and cheaper hosting while keeping what already works.

Will my site show up on Google?

We build every site with search basics in place: fast load times, clean structure, local business details, and pages for the services you offer. Ranking also takes time and content, and we can help with that too.

Can a custom app connect to our other software?

Usually, yes. We design custom tools to connect to the systems you already rely on.
