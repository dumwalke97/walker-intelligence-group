The problem we solve

Leadership teams are being told they need an AI strategy, usually by people selling something. It is hard to know what is real, what is safe, and what is worth the money.

We give you an honest outside view. We look at your operations, your data, and your goals, and tell you where AI will make a measurable difference and where it will not. Then, unlike most advisors, we can build what we recommend.

What we build for you

Technology and readiness assessment

A plain-English review of your current tools, data, and processes, and how ready they are for AI.

Opportunity mapping

A short list of AI and automation opportunities ranked by return, cost, and risk.

Tool and vendor guidance

Recommendations based on your data sensitivity and budget, so you do not overpay or put data at risk.

AI policy and guardrails

Simple rules for how your team can use AI safely, so adoption does not create new risk.

Ongoing advisory

A trusted partner to check in with as the technology changes, without a full-time hire.

Common projects

  • A leadership workshop that ends with a prioritized AI roadmap
  • A review of AI tools your team is already using, with a safe-use policy
  • A second opinion on a vendor proposal before you sign
  • Quarterly check-ins to adjust the plan as tools and costs change

Questions we hear

What do we get at the end?

A written roadmap with prioritized opportunities, expected impact, rough costs, and clear next steps. Not a slide deck full of buzzwords.

Do you only advise, or do you build too?

Both. We are an AI consulting and implementation firm. You can take the plan and run with it, or have us build it.

Are you tied to any AI vendor?

No. We recommend what fits your needs and data, not what pays us a commission.

Is this only for large companies?

No. Most of our work is with small and mid-sized organizations that need practical answers, not an enterprise program.
