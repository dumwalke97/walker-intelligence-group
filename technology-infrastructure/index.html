Technology Infrastructure

A secure foundation your business can rely on.

Cloud setup, backups, security, and a clear picture of what you have. We organize your technology so it is safe, reliable, and ready to grow.

The problem we solve

Many businesses run on technology that grew one decision at a time. Shared passwords, files in three places, no tested backups, and nobody sure what is still being paid for. It works until it does not.

We take inventory, close the obvious risks, and set up a clean, documented foundation. It is also the step that makes new tools, automation, and AI safe to add later.

What we build for you

Technology audit

A full inventory of your systems, accounts, devices, and subscriptions, with a plain list of what is at risk.

Cloud setup and migration

Move files and email to well-organized cloud services with the right permissions.

Backups and recovery

Automatic backups that are actually tested, so a lost laptop or ransomware is an inconvenience and not a disaster.

Security assessment and hardening

Multi-factor login, access controls, and basic protections that stop the most common attacks.

Scalable systems

Infrastructure that grows with you, without big upfront hardware costs.

Common projects

  • Moving a team off a local file server and onto organized cloud storage
  • Setting up multi-factor login and a password manager for the whole team
  • Tested backups for key systems with a simple recovery plan
  • A technology audit before a merger, new hire wave, or big software change

Questions we hear

Are you a managed IT provider?

We focus on getting your foundation right and supporting the systems we set up. For day-to-day help desk needs, we can work alongside your existing IT provider.

How do we know if we are at risk?

Start with an audit. Most businesses find a few high-risk gaps that are quick and inexpensive to fix.

Do we need this before adding new technology?

Often, yes. New software, automation, and AI are only as safe as the data and access rules behind them. A solid foundation makes every project faster and safer.

Can you work with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Yes. We work with both and help you get far more out of the one you already pay for.
