AI Integration

AI integration that does real work for your team.

We find the places where AI actually saves time in your business. Then we build the tools, connect them to your systems, and keep them running. No demos that go nowhere.

The problem we solve

Most businesses have tried ChatGPT. Very few have turned it into something the whole team uses every day. The gap is not the AI. It is the work of fitting it into your real processes, your data, and your security rules.

That is the part we do. We look at how work moves through your business, pick the tasks where AI gives a clear return, and build tools your people can use without training manuals.

What we build for you

Document review and extraction

Pull key details out of contracts, invoices, applications, and forms, and drop them into the systems you already use.

Summaries and drafting

Turn long emails, call notes, and reports into short summaries and first drafts your team can approve and send.

Internal AI assistants

A private assistant that answers questions from your own policies, manuals, and past work, so people stop digging through folders.

AI inside your existing tools

Add AI steps to the software you already pay for, instead of adding another app to log into.

Common projects

  • An intake process where AI reads incoming PDFs and fills in your CRM or spreadsheet
  • A quoting assistant that drafts proposals from your past jobs and price list
  • An inbox helper that sorts, tags, and drafts replies to common customer questions
  • A searchable knowledge base built from your SOPs and training documents

Questions we hear

Is our data safe if we use AI?

Yes, if it is set up correctly. We choose tools and settings based on how sensitive your data is, keep your information out of public model training, and limit who can access what. Your data stays yours.

Do we need to hire someone technical to run it?

No. We build, host, and maintain what we deliver. Your team just uses it.

How long does an AI project take?

Most first projects take a few weeks from kickoff to something your team is using. We start small, prove the value, and then expand.

What if AI is not a good fit for us?

Then we will tell you. Sometimes simple automation solves the problem better and for less money.
