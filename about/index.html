About

A partner who plans it and builds it.

Walker Intelligence Group is a technology consulting and implementation firm based in Indianapolis. We help organizations figure out where modern technology, automation, and AI fit, then we build, deploy, and support the solution.

Meet the founder

Walker Intelligence Group was founded in 2026 by Dustin Walker, a technology leader with more than 25 years of experience implementing, administering, and leading technology for organizations. Over that career he has worked hands-on across many roles and many generations of technology, from running the systems businesses depend on every day to leading the teams and projects that put new ones in place.

Dustin holds a degree in Computer and Information Technology from Purdue University. That combination of formal training and decades of real-world delivery is why WIG can both advise and build. He has seen plenty of technology waves come and go, and he knows the difference between hype and tools that actually change how a business runs.

He started Walker Intelligence Group because he is passionate about helping small and medium-sized businesses see what is possible with technology and innovation, including AI. Large companies have whole departments for this. Most growing businesses do not. WIG gives them a senior, hands-on partner who explains things in plain English and then gets the work done.

  • 25+ years in technology implementation, administration, and leadership
  • Degree in Computer and Information Technology, Purdue University
  • Focused on small and mid-sized businesses

What we believe

Plain English, no hype

You should understand what you are buying and why. We explain things clearly and tell you when new technology is not the answer.

Build, don't just advise

A plan only matters if it gets done. We build, launch, host, and support what we recommend.

Your data stays yours

We choose tools and settings based on how sensitive your information is, and we keep it out of public AI training and third-party hands.

Start small, prove value

We begin with a focused project that pays off quickly, then grow from there.
