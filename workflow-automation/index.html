Workflow Automation

Workflow automation that gives your team hours back.

Data entry, copy and paste between systems, chasing approvals, building the same report every week. We automate the repetitive work so your people can focus on the work that needs them.

The problem we solve

Every business has a few processes held together by spreadsheets, email threads, and one person who knows how it all works. They are slow, error-prone, and they break when that person is out.

We map those processes, find the steps a computer should be doing, and build automations that run on their own. You get faster turnaround, fewer mistakes, and a process that does not depend on memory.

What we build for you

Data entry and syncing

Move information between your CRM, accounting, scheduling, and spreadsheets automatically, so it is entered once and correct everywhere.

Routing, notifications, and follow-ups

New lead, new order, overdue invoice. The right person gets notified and the follow-up goes out on time, every time.

Automated reporting

Reports and dashboards that build themselves on a schedule, instead of someone assembling them by hand.

Paper and email to digital

Replace paper forms and email chains with online forms and tracked workflows.

Common projects

  • Web form leads that land in your CRM, get assigned, and get a same-day reply
  • Invoices and receipts that are captured, categorized, and sent to accounting
  • A weekly sales or operations report delivered to your inbox every Monday
  • Onboarding checklists that kick off automatically when a new client signs

Questions we hear

What tools can you automate?

Most modern business software can be connected, including Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, QuickBooks, popular CRMs, and anything with an API or email. If it has no connection point, we will find a workaround or tell you upfront.

Will automation replace my staff?

It replaces tasks, not people. The goal is to free your team from busywork so they can spend time on customers and growth.

What happens if an automation breaks?

We monitor what we build and fix issues as part of ongoing support. You are not left with a black box.
