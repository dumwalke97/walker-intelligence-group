Cost Optimization

Spend less on technology. Get more from it.

We find the software you are paying for and not using, the overlapping tools, and the manual work that automation can replace. Then we help you cut it.

The problem we solve

Software costs creep up quietly. A new subscription for every team, licenses for people who left, two tools that do the same job, and cloud bills nobody reviews.

We review what you pay for, what you actually use, and where people are doing work by hand that software could do. You get a clear list of savings and help making the changes.

What we build for you

Subscription audit

A full list of your software and services, who uses them, and what each one really costs.

Tool consolidation

Replace overlapping tools with fewer, better ones, and cancel what is not pulling its weight.

Cloud right-sizing

Match your cloud and hosting spend to what you actually need.

Automation for savings

Replace labor-intensive manual processes with automation that pays for itself.

ROI-ranked investment plan

When you do spend, put money into the technology investments with the fastest return.

Common projects

  • Finding unused licenses and duplicate tools across departments
  • Moving a website or app to hosting that costs a fraction of the old plan
  • Replacing an expensive niche tool with features you already own
  • Automating a manual process to free up hours every week

Questions we hear

How much can we save?

It depends on your setup, so we will not promise a number upfront. The audit gives you a specific list of savings before you commit to any changes.

Will cutting tools disrupt our team?

We plan changes carefully, move data for you, and help your team switch over so work keeps moving.

Is this a one-time project?

The audit is one-time. Many clients have us review costs once or twice a year as their needs change.

Can savings fund new projects?

Often, yes. Cutting waste is a common way to pay for a first automation or modernization project without a new budget line.
